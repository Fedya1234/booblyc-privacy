Applies to the games by BooblycTD: Rowly: Word Association Game (com.booblyc.word.association.puzzle.game) and Wordaire: Word Solitaire (com.booblyc.word.solitaire.cards.game).

The games have no user accounts. Game progress is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.

To delete analytics and diagnostics data linked to your device, email fedyadanilov@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request, the game name, and, if possible, the player ID shown in the game Settings. We delete the associated data within 30 days and confirm by email. Data that third-party ad networks hold is subject to their policies (see our privacy policy); you can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
